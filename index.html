How the numbers are figured

  • Pen Total = Invoice Total + Byr Com + Haul/Chg for the cut. If Invoice Total is blank it uses Hd × Avg Wt × $/cwt. If a sheet's Total already includes the commission, it is taken out first so it isn't counted twice.
  • Cost/Hd = Pen Total ÷ Hd.
  • Avg $/cwt = Pen Total ÷ Tot Wt × 100, using the invoice's Tot Wt (or Hd × Avg Wt if Tot Wt is blank).
  • Haul bills can be spread over every cut by head or put on one cut.
  • Days = (End Wt − Avg Wt) ÷ Gain/Day, rounded up. Out Date = purchase date + days.
  • Gain $ = lbs gained × cost of gain, plus yardage × days.
  • Interest on the cattle and vet for the full period, and on half the gain cost (it is spent gradually).
  • Death loss = death % × (cattle cost + half the gain cost), the extension-service method. Each cut uses the base death loss unless you pick its own Death % (0% to 20%) from that cut's list; a cut set above the base shows in orange, below it in green.
  • Breakeven $/cwt = Total $/Hd ÷ pay weight × 100, where pay weight = End Wt less shrink.
  • ± $/Hd shows only when you enter an expected sale price.
  • Steers outside the steer weight range are flagged, because the default gain may not hold there.
